01 - Welcome
Welcome to the heart
of Bristol

Housed in an iconic 18-storey high-rise, Majestic Hotels, Bristol places you at the centre of one of England's most captivating cities. Our hotel is within walking distance of a variety of popular attractions and just steps from public transport, so getting around is effortless.

In addition to a prime harbour location, we offer 176 modern rooms and suites with signature bedding, 24-hour room service and unlimited free Wi-Fi. Enjoy gastropub-style food at our Collage Restaurant, drinks overlooking the water, and helpful touches such as three-hour express laundry.

02 - The Setting
An address
like no other

Only 20 minutes from Bristol Airport and less than a ten-minute walk from some of the city's finest attractions, the hotel is a perfect base from which to explore. The Airport Flyer bus stops right outside, with a variety of other transport options close by.

Rising above the waterside, our rooms and suites frame scenic views over the harbour and the city skyline - the calm counterpoint to a day spent along the quays below.

07 - Explore Bristol
Moments from the door

Step outside and the city is already yours. From the harbourside on our doorstep to Brunel's engineering marvels and the West End stage, the finest of Bristol is a short walk away — distances below are from the hotel entrance.

0.10 mi

Bristol Hippodrome

Bristol's West End theatre and one of the largest stages in the country - catch the latest musical production.

0.15 mi

Bristol Aquarium

An undersea journey through nature-themed displays and a giant botanical house of exotic species.

0.18 mi

Bristol Harbour

The vibrant Harbourside, lined with trendy restaurants, bars and wonderful scenic views.

0.48 mi

Bristol Museum & Art Gallery

Vibrant collections of art and natural history spanning the last billion years of Earth's story.

0.54 mi

SS Great Britain

One of the UK's top attractions - Brunel's 1843 ship that began international passenger travel.

0.65 mi

Cabot Circus

The city's ultimate shopping hub - high-street and designer brands beneath a shell-shaped glass roof.

1.36 mi

Clifton Suspension Bridge

Brunel's architectural landmark, beautifully lit in the evening above the Avon Gorge.
